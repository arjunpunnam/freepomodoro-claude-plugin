# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** November 6, 2026

Free Pomodoro is operated by Integration Insights Inc. ("we", "us"). This policy explains what we collect when you use the focus timer at www.freepomodoro.io and the Free Pomodoro connector for Claude (together, the "Service"), why we collect it, and the choices you have.

Questions? Email us at contact@integrationinsightsinc.com.

## What we collect

**Account information.** You sign in to Free Pomodoro with Google. When you do, Google shares your name and email address with us. We never see your Google password.

**Timer and plan data.** When you start a focus session, break or focus plan, on our website or through Claude, we save the details needed to run it: its length, the label or plan name you give it, task names and lengths, and when it was created.

**Usage and device data.** We use Cloudflare to host the Service and Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand how the site is used. Cloudflare processes technical information such as IP address, browser type and pages visited to deliver and protect the Service. Cloudflare Web Analytics does not use cookies to track you.

**Cookies.** We use cookies only to keep you signed in. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

We don't collect payment information.

## Using Free Pomodoro with Claude

If you connect Free Pomodoro to Claude (Anthropic's AI assistant), you sign in with your Free Pomodoro account, and Claude can then start timers, breaks and focus plans for you.

- **What we receive from Claude:** only the details of each timer request, such as length, label, plan name and task names. We don't receive your conversations with Claude.
- **What Claude receives from us:** a confirmation and a link to the timer. The connector does not give Claude your timer history or account details.
- **Sign-in:** the connection uses OAuth, so Claude never sees your password. You can disconnect Free Pomodoro at any time in Claude's connector settings.

Anthropic's handling of your conversations is covered by Anthropic's own privacy policy, not this one.

## How we use your information

- To provide the Service: run your timers and plans, and keep you signed in.
- To keep the Service secure and fix problems.
- To contact you about your account or important changes to the Service.

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't use your data for advertising.

## Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that help us run the Service, and only as needed for that:

- **Cloudflare** — hosting, security and web analytics.
- **Google** — sign-in.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of our users or the Service.

## How long we keep it

We keep your account information, timers and plans for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, we delete your personal information within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep it.

## Your choices and rights

You can ask us to:

- give you a copy of your data,
- correct it,
- delete your account and data.

Email contact@integrationinsightsinc.com and we'll respond within 30 days. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you may have additional rights under local law.

## Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS) and rely on Cloudflare's infrastructure to protect your information. No online service is completely secure, but we work to keep your data safe.

## Children

The Service isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

## Changes to this policy

If we make significant changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page and let signed-in users know on the site.
